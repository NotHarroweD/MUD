# Playtest plan

## Scenarios

1. New player wins after recognizing and bracing the Cinder Burst.
2. New player loses after ignoring the defensive response.
3. Player receives common loot, compares it, and equips an upgrade.
4. Player forces and inspects rare loot.
5. Player earns level two, selects a trait, and understands the next goal.

## Questions

- Are the active enemy intent and player actions readable without a wall of instructions?
- Does Brace feel useful but not mandatory every turn?
- Does the loot reveal communicate rarity and an equipment decision?
- Does a rare item feel distinctly valuable?
- Is the next goal specific and motivating?
- Do buttons, keyboard focus, responsive layout, and canvas remain usable at desktop and mobile sizes?

## Recording standard

Separate actual browser observations, test results, and untested assumptions in `progress.md` and `docs/NEXT_MILESTONES.md`.
